About the studio

Emstene Couture is a fashion studio in Westlands, Nairobi, led by designer Sara Githua. We make outfits to measure for individuals, wedding parties and events.

Brands have trusted us with their event looks, including a L’Oréal product launch and the hosts of She Can Drive Kenya.
